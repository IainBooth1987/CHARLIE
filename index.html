Foundations first

Each subject starts with the building blocks Reception teachers focus on — subitising in maths, and phonics in the same order as school (Little Wandle) for English.

Short, playful rounds

Rounds are 8 questions with a "brick break" halfway that gets children moving away from the screen.

Charlie's Tower

Every finished round wins 2–4 bricks (more stars, more bricks). Children choose the colour, size and spot for each brick and build their own tower, castle or town. Special pieces like a door, tree and crown unlock as they earn more.

Talk about it

Ask "How did you know?" when they answer — explaining what they saw builds real number sense.

No computer voice

Children read the screen with you and say sounds themselves, as they do in class. Settings are behind the 🔒 in the corner — press and hold.
